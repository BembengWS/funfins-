FUNFINS+ WAVE / NOTCH BLADE. The Wave / Notch Blade features a distinctive wave/notch upper profile designed to provide a responsive and dynamic kicking experience.

Its unique geometry is designed around responsive flex, direct power transfer and precise blade control, giving the diver a more immediate connection between each kick and forward movement.

03 — FIN SHAPE / FUNCTION & ADVANTAGES

WAVE / NOTCH
POWER + RESPONSE.

This design features a distinctive wave/notch pattern at the upper section of the blade.

CHARACTERISTICS
  • Faster response during kicking.
  • More lively blade feel.
  • Suitable for frog kicks, dolphin kicks, and quick directional changes.
  • Can provide a more direct power response.
ADVANTAGES
  • Highly responsive.
  • Good power transfer during stronger kicks.
  • Good control for active kicking techniques.
  • Quick acceleration.
POTENTIAL DRAWBACK

Can feel more demanding when combined with a stiff blade material. May feel less smooth for relaxed, long-distance kicking compared with a more rounded design.

SIMPLE POSITIONING

POWER + RESPONSE

Fast response, powerful thrust, aggressive kick.

IMPORTANT NOTE

The blade shape alone does not determine the final performance of the fins.

The actual feel and performance are also strongly influenced by material, blade thickness, number of laminate layers, fiber orientation, rails, foot pocket, blade length, blade width, taper, and flex zone position and length.
