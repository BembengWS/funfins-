The Bulb / Rounded-Lobed profile is designed around efficient propulsion, controlled movement and a consistent kicking response. Its rounded-lobed geometry creates a distinctive character while supporting stable and measured blade movement.

CHARACTERISTICS
  • Efficient — designed to support efficient propulsion through a controlled and consistent kick cycle.
  • Controlled Flex — provides a measured flex response that allows the blade to load and recover progressively.
  • Balanced Power — delivers a controlled combination of propulsion and efficiency without an overly aggressive response.
  • Stable Movement — maintains predictable blade movement throughout the kicking cycle for consistent underwater control.
  • Smooth Energy Transfer — helps transfer kicking input into forward movement with a controlled and progressive response.
  • Controlled Recovery — returns progressively after each kick, helping maintain a steady rhythm and repeatable movement.
  • Endurance Focused — designed around a consistent kicking experience that suits longer periods of controlled underwater movement.
ADVANTAGES
  • Efficient Propulsion — supports steady forward movement while maintaining a controlled kicking rhythm.

  • Consistent Kick Cycle — the controlled flex and recovery help create a predictable movement pattern.
